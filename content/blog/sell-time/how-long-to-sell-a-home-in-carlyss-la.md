## What Happens After You Accept An Offer

Once you accept an offer, the clock doesn't stop. Here is what usually happens next:

- **Inspection period, about 7 to 14 days.** The buyer's inspector goes through the home, and the buyer may ask for repairs or a credit.
- **Appraisal, about 2 to 3 weeks.** If the buyer is financing, the lender orders an appraisal to confirm the value.
- **Loan approval and title work.** The lender finishes underwriting while the title company clears the title.
- **Closing.** Most financed sales close about 30 to 45 days after the offer is accepted. Cash sales can close much faster.
