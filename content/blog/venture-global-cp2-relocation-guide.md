If you are moving to Southwest Louisiana to work at Venture Global CP2, one of your first questions is probably simple:

**Where should I live?**

CP2 is being built in Cameron Parish, south of Lake Charles. There are not many homes close to the plant, so most workers will live farther north and drive to work.

For many workers, that means looking at places like Sulphur, Carlyss, Lake Charles, Westlake, Moss Bluff, Hackberry, Iowa, or Vinton.

This guide will help you understand the area, the drive, home prices, renting versus buying, and what to think about before you move. If you are new to the area in general, our [guide to moving to Lake Charles](/blog/moving-to-lake-charles) covers the bigger picture. And if you are coming off the Woodside job near Carlyss (or heading there next), see our [Woodside LNG Relocation Guide](/blog/woodside-lng-relocation-guide).

## What Is Venture Global CP2?

CP2 is a large LNG export plant being built by Venture Global in Cameron Parish.

It is being built on the east side of the Calcasieu Ship Channel near Venture Global's Calcasieu Pass plant. Part of the project also includes facilities on nearby Monkey Island.

This is a very large project.

Venture Global says CP2 is expected to have about **7,500 construction workers at peak construction**.

Once the plant is running, the company expects about **400 direct permanent CP2 jobs**. Venture Global also says the project should support about 3,000 new jobs in Louisiana.

So if you are moving here for CP2, you are not alone.

Thousands of workers and families will be making the same move.

## How Long Will CP2 Construction Last?

This is not a short project.

Venture Global started site work in 2025.

As of August 2026, the company says CP2 is still on schedule to produce its first LNG in the **second half of 2027**.

Construction will not simply end the day the first LNG is produced.

There are different parts of the project being built and brought online over time. Venture Global has also started work toward a possible expansion of CP2.

That matters when you are deciding where to live.

If you expect to be here for several years, it may make sense to compare buying a home with paying rent or staying in an extended-stay hotel.

## How Far Is CP2 From Lake Charles?

CP2 is south of the Lake Charles area in Cameron Parish.

A federal study for the project estimated that Lake Charles is about a **one-hour commute** from the terminal.

The same study named **Lake Charles, Sulphur, and Carlyss** as places that are within a reasonable commute for workers who need housing.

Your actual drive can change based on where you live, where you enter the job site, road work, weather, and shift traffic.

That is why we would not choose a home based only on what a map app says at 10:00 in the morning.

If you are working long shifts, an extra 15 or 20 minutes each way can matter.

## Where Should You Live If You Work At CP2?

There is no one right answer.

The best place depends on your family, your budget, and how much driving you are willing to do.

Here are some of the main areas to look at.

### Sulphur and Carlyss

For many families working at CP2, Sulphur and Carlyss are good places to start looking.

They are on the west side of the Calcasieu River.

That means you can head south toward Cameron Parish without having to cross the I-10 bridge through Lake Charles each day.

You also have grocery stores, restaurants, schools, medical care, and other things you may need every day.

Carlyss is south of Sulphur and can put you even closer to the Highway 27 route toward Cameron Parish.

The federal CP2 study specifically listed Sulphur and Carlyss as reasonable places for workers to live.

## What Do Homes Cost Near CP2?

Home prices are different from town to town.

They also change based on the age of the home, neighborhood, lot size, condition, and how much land comes with it.

Current market data gives us a good idea of the differences.

As of summer 2026, Realtor.com showed these median asking prices:

| Area | Median asking price |
| --- | --- |
| Sulphur | about $229,000 |
| Carlyss | about $242,000 |
| Lake Charles | about $252,000 |
| Westlake | about $229,000 |
| Moss Bluff | about $272,000 |
| Iowa | about $268,000 |
| Vinton | about $183,000 |

These are median listing prices, not the price every home sells for.

You can find homes above and below those numbers.

This is why it helps to compare areas instead of only setting a price limit.

A $250,000 home in one town may give you a very different home, lot, or drive than $250,000 in another town.

## Should You Rent Or Buy While Working At CP2?

There is no answer that works for everyone.

If you are coming here for six months or one year, buying a house may not make sense.

If you expect to be here for several years, it is worth running the numbers.

When you rent, your payment gives you a place to live.

When you buy, part of your payment may help you build equity in the home.

That does **not** mean buying is always better.

There are costs to buying and selling a home.

You also have to think about repairs, insurance, taxes, and what you will do with the home when your work here is finished.

But if you are going to spend thousands of dollars every month on housing for three, four, or five years, it is worth comparing both choices.

Do not assume renting is your only option just because you are here for work.

## Can You Buy A House If You Get Per Diem?

Yes.

Receiving per diem does not stop you from getting a mortgage.

The important part is understanding which income can actually be used to qualify.

If your per diem is **tax-free**, it cannot be used as qualifying income for the mortgage.

Your lender will look at your taxable qualifying income.

If money you call per diem is actually being paid to you as taxable income, it may be treated differently.

Your lender will need to look at your pay stubs, W-2s, employment history, and the loan program you are using.

So do not guess based on what another worker told you.

Have a lender look at your actual pay before you decide how much house you can afford. You can [start a pre-approval here](/get-pre-approved).

## What If My Employer Is In Another State?

Working for a company based in another state does not automatically stop you from buying a home in Louisiana.

The lender is going to look at your employment, income, and whether your job allows you to live in the home you are buying.

For CP2 workers, the important thing is being able to show that your employment and your move to Southwest Louisiana make sense together.

If you are relocating for the job, tell your lender that from the beginning.

## What About Schools?

If you are bringing kids with you, do not pick an area based on the town name alone.

The exact home address can decide which school your child attends.

Calcasieu Parish School Board has an official **Find My School Zone** tool, linked from the [Calcasieu Parish School Board website](https://www.cpsb.org), that lets parents check the school assigned to an address.

That is better than trusting a home listing or assuming every home in Sulphur or Lake Charles goes to the same schools.

If schools matter to your move, check the exact address before you make an offer.

## What Should You Do Before Your Start Date?

**First, find out how long you expect to work here.**
A one-year job and a five-year job can lead to very different housing choices.

**Next, figure out how much driving you are willing to do.**
If you work 10-hour or 12-hour shifts, your drive matters even more.

**Then talk to a lender before you start looking at homes.**
This is especially important if your pay includes per diem, overtime, bonuses, or other types of pay that may need to be reviewed.

**After that, come see the area if you can.**
Sulphur, Carlyss, Lake Charles, Moss Bluff, and Hackberry do not feel the same. You can look at pictures online, but that will not tell you what the drive feels like or what it is like to live there every day.

**Finally, work with someone who knows Southwest Louisiana.**
Moving here on a job start date is different from buying a house when you already live here.
